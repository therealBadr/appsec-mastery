# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique work and not people, and keep discussion on topic. Harassment,
discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated. Requests for help attacking systems you do not own are off topic and will be closed.

Maintainers may remove comments or ban participants who violate these rules. Report problems by opening a private
security advisory on the Security tab or contacting the maintainer through the profile linked in the repository.
