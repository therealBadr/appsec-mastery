# Responsible use

This is an offensive-security-aware curriculum. Understanding attacks is how defenders learn, and it is also how people get into trouble. A few rules apply to everything here.

!!! danger "The lab-only rule"
    Run attacks only against systems you own or have explicit written permission to test. Practice in your own virtual machines,
    on intentionally vulnerable applications, or on platforms built for it (CTFs, training labs, bug bounty programs within
    their stated scope). If you are unsure whether you are allowed, you are not.

## What I publish

- Techniques, mechanisms and defenses, with proof-of-concept limited to lab targets.
- No credentials, private keys, real hostnames or real people's data. Anything from my lab that looks like a secret is redacted, and the repository scans every commit for them.
- No working attacks against named real-world services or unpatched, undisclosed vulnerabilities.

## If you find a real vulnerability

Follow the owner's disclosure policy. If none exists, report privately and give them reasonable time to fix it. Never test further than needed to confirm the issue.
